Postlab Q2:

Increasing the window size has two effects. First, increasing the filter size leads to a lower pass threshold. This means that the range of frequencies above this threshold increases and gets fitlered out.
Basically, omitting the noise better. However, one has to be careful not to filter out the actual underlying dynamics of the system.
Second, the window size determines the phase shift of the fitlered signal compared to the raw signal. With increasing window size the phase shift increases as well. 
Based on the maximum delay of 0.16 seconds one should choose n=20 at max.
